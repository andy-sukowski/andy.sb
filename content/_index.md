## My Linux Setup

A few years ago, I completely wiped Windows from my SSD, replacing it with Ubuntu.
Over the years, I've distrohopped to Arch, Gentoo (took way too long to compile
programs) and finally to Void Linux.
I've used Gnome, KDE, qtile, i3 and now DWM from [suckless.org][suckless].
My workflow is primarily command-line based, so I frequently use Neovim,
ffmpeg, ImageMagick, GnuPG, [pass][7] and the *nix coreutils.
